          How to pair ↗
          Setup and privacy

          In Glance Settings, choose Browser Focus → Enable browser connection. Then choose Connect to Glance in the extension and approve pairing on your Mac.

          Each browser grants its own website and usage permissions. Your activity stays on your devices.
