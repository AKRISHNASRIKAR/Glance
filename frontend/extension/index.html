01 / STAY WITH YOUR SESSION

A quiet page.
A clear countdown.

Sites on your list are blocked while focus is running. The blocked page keeps the timer in the center, with Manage list as its only action.

Pauses and breaks allow access. Closing the popup does not stop the session.

02 / ONE TIMER, TOGETHER

Your Mac leads.
Chrome follows.

When paired, Glance owns the timer, deadline, website rules and shared settings. Start or pause from either place. Reload Chrome and it reconnects to the app's session.

Without the app, use a standalone browser timer. Connection loss keeps the last confirmed deadline while the extension retries.

Your time, kept local.

Insights you can choose.
Data you can control.

Usage tracking is optional. See active website time, completed focus sessions and blocked-page attempts. Pair with your Mac to bring the stats into Glance.

Active time, not every tab.

Only the active tab in a focused browser window counts. Idle time, private windows, browser pages and local addresses are excluded.

Domains and durations.

No page contents, URL paths, queries or keystrokes. Activity stays in your browser and, after pairing, on your Mac.

Keep what helps.

Exclude websites, pause tracking, export your local data or delete it. Activity is retained for 30 days.

Try it on your own browser.

A small setup.
Then get back to it.

This is a developer preview. It is not on the Chrome Web Store and no public install package is available yet.

  1. 1

    Load the extension

    In a developer checkout, open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, and load the browser-extension/ folder unpacked.

  2. 2

    Choose your websites

    Add a domain and approve its browser permission. Start a standalone session now, or pair with the Mac app.

  3. 3

    Bring your Mac along

    Enable Browser Focus in Glance Settings. Connect from the extension and approve pairing on your Mac.
